- **The feature pipeline routes by complexity, and brain reads are search-first.** Triage's `complexity:` now selects a lane: `S` runs the **fast lane** (main-thread research instead of an Explore agent, inline text plan approval instead of the `plan-html` server, and the second-opinion subagent skipped unless the plan names ≥3 files or the change is user-visible); `M`/`L` run the **full lane** exactly as before. Both lanes produce identical artifacts, stage transitions, and metrics events — a skipped second opinion is recorded in the plan's `## Second opinion` section, and no `second_opinion` metric is emitted for it. The plan drafter now runs the recurring second-opinion findings as a pre-dispatch self-check (success-criteria coverage, vacuous verification, blast radius, runaway holes), and the reviewer's `must_fix` is reserved for correctness, coverage gaps, and vacuous verification — style goes to `nice_to_have` — to cut the double reviewer round that telemetry showed on 100% of plans. The unfiltered `brain-read` (up to 8000 tokens) no longer runs mid-pipeline: research, plan, and implement use search-first lookups (`brain-search` headlines, full-read of the top 1–2 hits, scaled by complexity), and the exec plan is the deterministic carrier — research writes distilled `### Prior lessons` bullets and a `### Conventions card` (build/lint/test commands verbatim from `AGENTS.md` plus the conventions the feature touches) into the plan's Research section, and later stages and the second-opinion subagent read those instead of re-querying the brain or re-reading `AGENTS.md`. Supersedes the skip-if-already-in-context brain guard and the `BRAIN_FILES` file-scoped `brain-read` introduced by the hive-brain changeset. `feature-loop`'s skill text is trimmed (metrics fallback prose compressed, non-final-phase rules consolidated into one section).
